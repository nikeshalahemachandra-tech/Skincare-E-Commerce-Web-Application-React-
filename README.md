✨ LuxeCream – Skincare E-Commerce Web Application

LuxeCream is a modern and responsive e-commerce web application designed for browsing and purchasing premium face and body skincare products. The platform provides an engaging shopping experience with product discovery, detailed product information, customer reviews, shopping cart management, and a streamlined checkout workflow.

📌 Project Overview

LuxeCream was developed to provide a clean, elegant, and user-friendly online shopping experience for skincare customers.

The application allows users to explore skincare collections, view product details, manage their shopping cart, and interact with product reviews and ratings.

🏠 Main Pages
Home

Introduces the LuxeCream brand with a promotional hero slider, featured products, customer testimonials, and calls-to-action.

Products

Allows customers to browse skincare products and explore different product categories.

Product Details

Provides detailed information including product descriptions, ingredients, pricing, ratings, and reviews.

Shopping Cart

Allows customers to add products, change quantities, remove items, and review their selected products.

Checkout

Provides a structured checkout workflow for completing purchases.

About Us

Introduces the LuxeCream brand and its skincare focus.

Contact

Provides customers with a way to contact the business.
